<br>There were times when I didn&#39;t want to talk about things I wasn&#39;t sure about. After submitting the documents way ahead of the scheduled deadline, I felt like something was fakely finished. And indeed, since last Monday, I&#39;ve woken up in the morning wondering, &quot;What should I do today?&quot; for the first time in a while. Still, I worked on the posts I had been writing in parallel, and in the afternoon, I went to Pyeongchang-dong. Lately, there hasn&#39;t been much music to listen to, so I just listen to Generation Electronica playlists. On my way home, if the sun has set and traffic is a bit heavy, I sometimes turn on a playlist of K-pop songs, bang on the steering wheel, and sing along until the outside echoes; but perhaps because the days are getting longer these days, I just don&#39;t feel like listening to K-pop. After spending the last few months wondering, &quot;Has it already been this long?&quot;, having some free time makes me feel even more disoriented. I stopped reading a book halfway through, and even after adding the tag filtering feature to my blog, I felt so lonely that I almost texted someone who popped up in my messenger friend recommendations. Just kidding. In spring, nothing beats flounder, and if the fine dust levels are bad, I just run on the treadmill.
