Last week, since my partner was on a business trip to Jeju, I headed there with the intention of hanging out and hanging out after work. Coincidentally, Yuseong also said he wanted to brainstorm some ideas for his work on his own, so the schedules aligned perfectly. I didn&#39;t make any grand plans; I just intended to go for a swim, run around, and eat some bracken yukgaejang. The sea, which I found after checking the tides, was still cold, but it wasn&#39;t crowded, so it was clear and pristine. Jeju in June, right before the rainy season, was nice—uncrowded, relaxed, and enjoyable, unlike midsummer. That doesn&#39;t mean I won&#39;t go in midsummer, though... Stepping Stone is opening again this year, so I definitely have to go.
