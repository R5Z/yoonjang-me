Still, when I was bored to the point of going crazy, I would eat out as if tomorrow were the weekend and deliberately stay up late. Sometimes that could be things like failing to choose a movie to watch or running on a course I&#39;ve never taken before. I just want to feel okay with whatever I do. For the past few days, I&#39;ve checked the library more than twice a day to see when the books I requested will be in stock. I ordered items for delivery and forgot about them; the day I went out without a bag was the happiest day.
